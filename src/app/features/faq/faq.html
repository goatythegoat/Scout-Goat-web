Is ScoutGoat free?

ScoutGoat is a program that is free to access for personal use; it also offers various versions and paid plans designed to implement enterprise-level features. There are currently three plans available: Free, Premium, and Enterprise.

What are the system requirements?

ScoutGoat is optimized specifically for Windows and linux operating systems. Thanks to its lightweight architecture, It was designed for low profile systems and requires minimal RAM and CPU resources.

How is my data protected?

Privacy is a core pillar of ScoutGoat. All critical security operations and configurations are processed locally on your machine, ensuring your sensitive data remains under your complete control without unnecessary external tracking.
